Frankenstein's Corpse Reviver

Description

A vibrant victual sure to raise any body from the dead.

Makes 20 ounces.

Ingredients

  • 15 ounces green chartreuse
  • 5 ounces apple brandy
  • 5 ounces fresh lime juice (5 limes)
  • 5 ounce dandelion simple syrup
  • green luster dust

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients in a large pitcher.
  2. Decant 2 oz into each test tube.
